## Hook formula

1. Start mid-action with speech in the first 0-5 s (first speech at 0.1-5 s in all 5 transcribed videos). Omar is already reacting: "I don't get it, man" (Oman), "careful, careful" (Cairo), "It's 5:30 am" (India).
2. Pack the first minute with the most striking images from later in the film, including **a flash-forward of Omar himself transformed or inside the experience** (`4UZaeH6A12Y@0:10` in the headdress; `IzSzEglLAcI@0:59` arms wide underground).
3. State the premise as a contrast or a personal doubt: buildings up vs down (`IzSzEglLAcI@0:10`), "it just doesn't feel entertaining to me" (`A7IAreQ4ZsI@0:44`), "We're creating the garbage, not them" (`zB0WvXN6TJ8@0:49`), a childhood-documentary memory (`4UZaeH6A12Y@0:11`).
4. List the open questions and invite the viewer along ("I'm here to find out with you", `cZRSEPWZa-8@1:04`).
5. Reset to the map or arrival piece-to-camera at 1:00-2:30.

The Basmala is spoken at or near the start in at least 3 videos (`4UZaeH6A12Y@0:04`, `A7IAreQ4ZsI@0:09`, and inside the Matmata cues). It sits inside the cold open, not before it.

## Structure & pacing

- 21-28 min, no YouTube chapters. The arc is: hook -> map/arrival -> host's home or workplace -> daily life and cultural rules -> history or context chapter -> participation -> climax event or end product -> short reflection -> logo.
- A **mid-film context chapter** changes texture: Barka fort and museum `A7IAreQ4ZsI@11:24-12:14`; museum panels `cZRSEPWZa-8@7:35`; Star Wars hotel `IzSzEglLAcI@19:22`; the NGO `zB0WvXN6TJ8@17:07`; the show-world montage `BA83kfPOz1w@19:01-20:11`.
- **Mid-roll calls to action** around the midpoint: subscribe animation `4UZaeH6A12Y@16:48`, like/subscribe overlay `A7IAreQ4ZsI@9:35`, spoken joke "just subscribe" `cZRSEPWZa-8@14:19`. A sponsor read (Noqta) appears in `A7IAreQ4ZsI@10:54-11:14`, and Noqta is also in the Qatar description.
- Measured: median visual-sequence length is 49-109 s, so scenes are long and conversational. Speech-free stretches are short (usually 12-40 s) and placed at transitions and the climax. The estimated cuts/min (14-18) saturates the estimator in 3 videos, so treat it only as "brisk".
- Endings are short: a reflection of 30-60 s, then the logo. There is no recap montage.

## Camera & composition

- Handheld or gimbal medium two-shots and walk-and-talks dominate (face frames are 23-43% of samples).
- Wide lens near faces, low angles against sky, frames within frames through doorways (`IzSzEglLAcI@3:47`) and vehicle windows (`zB0WvXN6TJ8@2:48`).
- Detail inserts at the moment a process is explained: beads `4UZaeH6A12Y@10:12`, barley `A7IAreQ4ZsI@8:16`, wood under a magnifier `cZRSEPWZa-8@18:47`, plastic flakes `zB0WvXN6TJ8@13:10`.
- Drones: oblique establishing shots plus straight-down punctuation; FPV-style tilted passes only in Oman (`A7IAreQ4ZsI@25:37`).
- Occasional staged or stylised set-ups: red sofas in a field (`BA83kfPOz1w@7:27`) and a lit night storytelling stage (`cZRSEPWZa-8@5:36`).
- Exact camera moves, transitions and cut points are not observable at storyboard resolution.

- A restrained, filmic grade: moderate saturation (0.30-0.43), low contrast (0.18-0.23), warm-leaning in 4 of 6 (warmth 0.06-0.14). Highlights are lifted and creamy, greens are olive or khaki, and skies are pale teal or hazy.

- The vintage-frame inserts carry their own, often more saturated or washed-out, archival look.

## Sound & narration

- Talk-led: speech coverage is 0.57-0.73 and 100-148 wpm (English subtitle words) in the five transcribed videos. Omar asks many questions (14-128 per video), mixing first person singular and plural ("I", "we") with direct address to the viewer ("Like you, when I first heard...", `zB0WvXN6TJ8@2:58`).
- Two registers: diary-style voiceover that bridges scenes ("We left Chad's capital and drove for hours", `4UZaeH6A12Y@1:55`) and live, often comic, reactions on camera ("Six hours?!", `4UZaeH6A12Y@1:38`).
- Speech gaps cluster at ritual or event climaxes (Chad dances 12:35-18:22; Oman finale 25:09-27:06). This is consistent with music or ambient sound carrying the spectacle. Audio was not analysed.

## When this approach fits a scene

- **A community practice with a clear event or product** (a festival, a match, a harvest, a craft chain) that the presenter can build toward and then take part in.
- **A single generous host** who can carry long two-shot conversations. Give them protagonist framing.
- **A topic surrounded by prejudice or mystery** that the presenter can voice in the hook and then dismantle (Cairo, Oman, India).
- **Multi-day stays** where night and dawn transitions (`4UZaeH6A12Y@2:18-4:17`, `IzSzEglLAcI@5:45-6:44`) give the film a natural rhythm.
- It fits less well for pure landscape or solo travel with no host, or for scenes without dialogue (the style depends on conversation).

## What NOT to copy

- Disgust or "first person ever to see this" framing (`zB0WvXN6TJ8@0:59`, the fair-skin remark at `4UZaeH6A12Y@0:38`) without the community's own counter-voice.
- Stacking a subscribe overlay and a sponsor read in the same five minutes (`A7IAreQ4ZsI@9:35`, `A7IAreQ4ZsI@10:54`).
- Illustrated or AI-looking allegory images inside an observational documentary (`cZRSEPWZa-8@6:06`) unless they are clearly labelled.
- Borrowed archive or show footage as filler, especially inside the vintage frame; check rights.
- Tilted FPV aerials on footage that was not shot for it.
- Hiding people by blurring in the edit instead of planning consent and coverage on location.

## Measurement caveats

- Frames are YouTube storyboard tiles (320x180, one every ~9.9 s). Individual cuts, transitions, camera moves, on-screen text content and fine detail are **not observable at storyboard resolution**. The cut-rate figures are Poisson estimates and saturate at ~18 cuts/min in 3 of 6 videos.
- **All language metrics (wpm, words in the first 30 s, questions, first-person and second-person rates, "hook words") are computed on English subtitles of Arabic (and some local-language) speech.** They measure the translation, not Omar's actual delivery. Word counts and pacing will differ from the Arabic audio.
- BA83kfPOz1w has **no transcript**: every speech and sound metric for it is 0 or unavailable, not a real finding.
- IzSzEglLAcI's subtitle timing is **corrupted**. 153 cues are packed into the first 120 s, including content that belongs to the 19:22-20:51 Star Wars segment, and the cues stop at 18:16. Its "276 words in the first 30 s" and "204 s speech-free ending" are artefacts.
- Speech-free runs are inferred from subtitle gaps. Music and ambience are assumed, not heard. Audio was not downloaded.
- Shot-type and style-mode shares (cinematic / documentary / personal) are heuristic, computed on low-resolution frames.
- Arabic on-screen text was identified by role (subtitle, map label, name caption, verse) but not fully translated.
