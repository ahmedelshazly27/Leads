## Signature

These appear in at least 3 of the 6 videos.

1. **Map graphics bridge every move between places (all 6).** Stylised maps come in three looks: a 3D satellite globe with a glowing route line, a parchment relief map, and an illustrated map. They appear at the start of the film and again at each relocation. Examples: `7YobC1Q40M0@1:19`, `7YobC1Q40M0@8:33`, `EBv2H0WjtsI@2:29`, `EBv2H0WjtsI@7:06`, `Ki_Cjln0dFg@6:19`, `Ki_Cjln0dFg@28:43`, `crZDPaE8Wn4@2:58`, `crZDPaE8Wn4@16:59`, `fR3X3oSn4-w@2:39`, `fR3X3oSn4-w@13:15`, `twMvQ64jom8@22:49`.
2. **Each new settlement gets a drone establishing shot, often straight down.** Packed rooftops or a whole town fill the frame before the cut to eye level. Examples: `7YobC1Q40M0@4:26`, `7YobC1Q40M0@16:17`, `EBv2H0WjtsI@6:56`, `crZDPaE8Wn4@9:53`, `Ki_Cjln0dFg@3:39`, `fR3X3oSn4-w@3:39`, `twMvQ64jom8@2:09`.
3. **The film opens on its most extreme image, then a fact or question.** There is no cold-open scene. A few seconds of the strangest visual come first, followed by VO with one thesis or question: "How long can you hold your breath underwater?", "96% ... did not reach it alone", "I've always wondered...". Examples: `7YobC1Q40M0@0:00` (underwater), `EBv2H0WjtsI@0:00` (Dallol colour field), `crZDPaE8Wn4@0:00` (alpenglow), `fR3X3oSn4-w@0:00`, `twMvQ64jom8@0:00`. Replay data backs this up: the 0:00 segment is the top replay peak in 5 of 6 videos.
4. **A later set-piece is teased inside the first 2 minutes.** Examples: hyena feeding at `EBv2H0WjtsI@0:20` (paid off at 15:50), rocket hall and a TV launch at `twMvQ64jom8@1:29` (paid off at Baikonur, 23:00 on), night fishing in a soundbite cluster at `7YobC1Q40M0@1:58`, archival expedition footage at `crZDPaE8Wn4@1:29`.
5. **Joe takes part next to a local guide, shot as a 50/50 two-shot.** Most coverage is walk-and-talk or standing two-shots with a host, and Joe joins the work. Examples: `EBv2H0WjtsI@7:35`, `crZDPaE8Wn4@12:22`, `Ki_Cjln0dFg@6:58`, `twMvQ64jom8@3:18`. Process participation: salt mining `EBv2H0WjtsI@3:08`, yurt raising `twMvQ64jom8@6:17`, underground oven `Ki_Cjln0dFg@17:16`, bread making `fR3X3oSn4-w@17:23`.

## Hook formula

1. **0–5 s:** the strangest or most colourful image in the film, with no presenter. Examples: underwater seagrass, a sulphur field, alpenglow.
2. **3–21 s:** VO gives one fact or one question. It is short and declarative and uses "we" ("We're with the Bajau...", "Welcome to Kazakhstan").
3. **20–90 s:** a sizzle of 6–10 best shots. It includes one people or performance moment and one shot from the finale (`twMvQ64jom8@1:59`, `EBv2H0WjtsI@0:20`).
4. **About 1:00–2:00:** a map plus a statement of the plan ("we'll start first from...", "We'll start with the least isolated of the three"). Then chapter 1 starts in the first location with travel logistics: airport, luggage, plane (`7YobC1Q40M0@2:28`, `crZDPaE8Wn4@2:28`, `fR3X3oSn4-w@26:00`).

## Structure & pacing

- **Location-by-location tour.** English uploads carry YouTube chapters (4–17), and each chapter works as a mini-episode: map, drone establishing shot, arrival on foot, host, a process or performance, then a wordless landscape out. The two Arabic-titled uploads have no chapters but follow the same order.
- **Escalation.** The last chapter is the biggest set-piece: night hyenas (EBv2H0WjtsI), Baikonur (twMvQ64jom8), a helicopter to Everest (crZDPaE8Wn4), Saint Helena (fR3X3oSn4-w), the breath-hold dive and elder (7YobC1Q40M0).
- **Speech-free montages close chapters.** Examples: `twMvQ64jom8@13:03-14:24`, `Ki_Cjln0dFg@40:01-41:45`, `7YobC1Q40M0@12:29-13:48`.
- **Runtime is long:** 20–44 minutes. Long pieces run flat in the middle: Oman's chapters blur into each other, and Kazakhstan's tech section drags.
- **Pacing cannot be measured** from storyboard frames (see caveats).

## Camera & composition

- Drone everywhere: top-down town patterns, high-oblique coastlines, and tiny figures in vast terrain (`EBv2H0WjtsI@5:27`, `twMvQ64jom8@13:23`).
- Low-angle, wide-lens hero frames of Joe and hosts against saturated sky (`crZDPaE8Wn4@12:22`, `EBv2H0WjtsI@2:58`, `Ki_Cjln0dFg@14:37`).
- Wide-angle selfie for vlog beats and sign-offs (`fR3X3oSn4-w@27:49`, `7YobC1Q40M0@18:55`, `crZDPaE8Wn4@19:47`).
- Symmetry and depth in architecture and industrial spaces (`Ki_Cjln0dFg@8:38`, `twMvQ64jom8@33:03`, `twMvQ64jom8@28:36`).
- Underwater wides where the subject allows (`7YobC1Q40M0@13:19-15:38`).
- Not observable at storyboard resolution: camera moves, gimbal versus handheld, focal lengths, transitions.

## Colour & grade

- Default look: high saturation with a cyan/teal push in skies, water and whites. Profiles tag it "cool tones, highly saturated" for 7YobC1Q40M0, EBv2H0WjtsI, crZDPaE8Wn4 and twMvQ64jom8, with mean saturation 0.45–0.50. Warm skin is kept against that push.
- Each place keeps its own palette inside that look: ochre and sulphur in Danakil, mint walls in Harar, orange dunes in Oman, misty green in Dhofar, overcast teal-grey on Saint Helena.
- Night sequences use a hard warm key against deep blue ambient (`EBv2H0WjtsI@16:30`, `Ki_Cjln0dFg@17:46`).
- Archival footage is left faded or black-and-white for contrast.

## Sound & narration

- There are two narration modes:
  - **Essay narrator:** written, third-person and "we", rich in facts. Used in 7YobC1Q40M0, Ki_Cjln0dFg and twMvQ64jom8, with 35–50% speech coverage and speech-free runs of 80–104 s.
  - **On-camera conversation:** dialogue-heavy and full of questions (53–84 questions per video). Used in crZDPaE8Wn4, fR3X3oSn4-w and EBv2H0WjtsI, with 60–78% speech coverage. Auto-captions mark [music] and [laughter] often (crZDPaE8Wn4 16/16, fR3X3oSn4-w 25/14).
- Music and ambience: in three videos Joe credits Epidemic Sound for most music and SFX. In EBv2H0WjtsI@20:50 he says location ambience is sometimes not recorded and is rebuilt from the library.
- The audio itself was not analysed. Music is inferred from speech gaps and caption markers.

## When this approach fits a scene

- **Community-access stories** where a fixer or local guide is on camera and you can join a craft, food or ritual. The two-shot-plus-inserts grammar needs a willing host.
- **Places with an unusual aerial signature:** stilt villages, walled cities, striped badlands, island chains. The film's establishing grammar depends on strong drone material.
- **Trips across several locations** that need a map bridge to stay readable.
- **One extraordinary set-piece** (night hyenas, abandoned hangar, breath-hold dive) that can be teased at 0:20 and paid off at the end.
- **Diegetic performances** (drums, dance, singing) that can carry long wordless stretches.
- **Less suited to:** quiet solo travel without hosts, or footage with no drone coverage.

## Variation across videos

- **Narration mode:** essay VO (Bajau, Oman, Kazakhstan) versus conversation-led (Sherpa, Islands, and partly Ethiopia).
- **Shape:** single subject (Bajau, Sherpa), two-part (Ethiopia), country tour (Oman, Kazakhstan), escalation triptych (Islands).
- **Chapters:** none on the Arabic-titled uploads, 4–17 on the English ones.
- **Ending:** a sponsor read (Epidemic Sound) after the sign-off in 3 videos (`7YobC1Q40M0@19:05`, `EBv2H0WjtsI@20:47`, `crZDPaE8Wn4@25:23`). The other 3 end on a reflective VO or a wordless landscape coda (`Ki_Cjln0dFg@42:40`, `fR3X3oSn4-w@32:47`, `twMvQ64jom8@35:32`).
- **Amount of selfie vlogging:** heavy on Saint Helena (fR3X3oSn4-w), minimal in Oman.
- **Grade:** Oman measures neutral and warmer, while the others are cool and saturated.

## What NOT to copy

- Cutting in broadcaster footage with the bug still visible (BBC, 60 Minutes, Al Jazeera, AP). Clear rights or leave it out.
- Shock inserts such as the pixelated body (`crZDPaE8Wn4@23:15`).
- Merging separate trips into one "journey" and only revealing it in the outro (`7YobC1Q40M0@19:14`).
- Long institutional or hosted segments that read as promotion (`twMvQ64jom8@16:32-21:19`), and in-story engagement bait ("if this video hits 1 million likes").
- Sponsor reads placed straight after the emotional climax.
- Unvaried locked-off two-shots running 50 s or more.
- Sticker-style "TOP!" graphics over otherwise cinematic material (`twMvQ64jom8@31:04`).
- Applying the heavy teal/saturation push to footage not exposed for it.

## Measurement caveats

- Frames are YouTube storyboard tiles at 320x180, one every ~10 s. Anything shorter than about 10 s falls between frames. Exact cuts, transitions, camera moves, lens choices and fine grade detail are not observable at storyboard resolution.
- `est_cuts_per_min` reads about 18 in every video because the estimator saturates (almost every sampled frame differs). Do not use it to compare pacing.
- Shot types, face fractions and style-mode mixes come from heuristics on low-res frames and are approximate.
- Audio is not analysed. Music and ambience are inferred from speech gaps and from auto-caption markers, which exist only in crZDPaE8Wn4 and fR3X3oSn4-w.
- Transcripts are English captions, partly auto-generated, for videos whose original narration is Arabic in at least two cases. Wording is approximate.
- Timestamps in this guide are storyboard tile times, accurate to about ±10 s.
