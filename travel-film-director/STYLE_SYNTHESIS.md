# Style synthesis: Joe Hattab · Omar Farooq · Yusufa Sey · Daniel Dalen

Built from **24 videos (6 per creator)**. For each video, every storyboard contact sheet was viewed (91 sheets in total), plus the measured profile and transcript. Per-creator guides with timestamp evidence are in `data/library/<creator>/style_notes.md`, with copies in `examples/style_notes/`. Per-video notes (351 timestamped observations) are in `examples/annotations/`.

All four creators are personal on-camera presenters. They differ in **what carries the film**:

| | Joe Hattab | Omar Farooq | Yusufa Sey | Daniel Dalen |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Carried by | Place and spectacle | One community, through participation | Access to people and stakes | The creator's own voice |
| Dominant mode (measured mix) | Cinematic 48% | Balanced 35/34/32 | Personal/documentary (visual-only for 4 of 6) | Personal 51% |
| Speech share | 57% (35–78) | 64% (57–73) | ~90% (2 videos measured) | 87% (74–98) |
| Narration | Written essay VO **or** on-camera conversation | Diary VO bridges plus live comic reactions | Pieces to camera, no VO | Live talk to camera, no VO |
| Structure | Location chapters, escalating to a finale set-piece | Arrival → host → daily life → context chapter → participation → climax | Day-by-day, payoff in the last third | Chronological diary, alternating work and leisure, closing reflection |
| Bridges between places | Map graphics (3D globe, route line) + top-down drone | Maps inside a vintage-TV frame + top-down drone | Black text cards ("THE NEXT DAY", times) | Bold yellow place/time cards |
| Grade | Saturated, cool teal push, warm skin | Restrained, warm, low contrast, nights left dark | Warm amber, changes with location | Warm, natural, practical-lit |
| Length | 20–44 min | 22–28 min | 13–30 min | 16–36 min |

## Shared grammar (use these whatever the mode)

1. **Burned-in subtitles on dialogue.** All four creators do this in every video. Their films work with the sound off.
2. **Open on the best of the film, then reset.** Each creator does a version of this (formulas below). None opens with a slow chronological start.
3. **A visible system for time and place.** Maps (Joe, Omar) or text cards (Daniel, Yusufa). The audience always knows where and when they are.
4. **Two-shot with a host, then detail inserts.** Joe and Omar build coverage this way: a 50/50 two-shot with a local, broken up by close-ups of hands, tools and food.
5. **Short speech-free breathers at transitions and climaxes.** Typically 12–40 s; Joe Hattab runs them up to 80–104 s.
6. **Endings are short.** A 30–60 s reflection or landscape coda, then out.

## Hook formulas

| Creator | Formula | Use it when your footage has… |
|---|---|---|
| **Joe Hattab** | 0–5 s: the strangest image, no presenter. 3–21 s: one fact or one question in VO. 20–90 s: a sizzle of 6–10 best shots, including one shot from the finale. ~1–2 min: map + "we'll start with…" | One extraordinary visual or set-piece, plus strong drone footage |
| **Omar Farooq** | Speech in the first 0–5 s, already reacting mid-action. Flash-forward of the presenter inside the experience. Premise stated as a contrast or personal doubt. "Find out with me". Reset to map/arrival at 1–2:30. | A participation moment (you trying something) and a clear question or prejudice to answer |
| **Yusufa Sey** | Visual lead-in without context (in the car, an arrival, a stakes overlay). Title card on black. Piece to camera: where you are and what has to happen. | Real stakes (a meeting, a deadline, a goal) and people with access |
| **Daniel Dalen** | 10–30 s of music with teaser shots that return later as payoffs. Then a casual spoken premise. | Lots of selfie talk and warm moments with friends |

## Scene playbook (what the director applies)

| Scene in your footage | Approach | Borrowed from |
|---|---|---|
| First arrival in a country or region | Map bridge with a route line, top-down drone establishing shot, eye-level arrival | Joe, Omar |
| New day or new city in a multi-day trip | Bold place/time card over a b-roll insert | Daniel, Yusufa |
| Landscape, aerial, golden hour | Speech-free montage 12–40 s on music; up to ~90 s for a chapter close | Joe |
| Conversation with a local or host | Locked or gimbal two-shot, detail inserts every few lines, burned-in subtitles | Joe, Omar |
| You doing the local activity (craft, food, ritual) | Participation beat in the second half; tease it in the hook | Omar, Joe |
| Context or history you can verify | VO over maps/archival inside a frame (only with rights) — needs facts in `trip_notes.md` | Joe, Omar |
| Meetings, car rides, logistics | Fly-on-the-wall at the table, car ride-along as connective tissue | Yusufa |
| Talking to camera, reflection | Short locked-off reflection covered with b-roll; end the film on it | Daniel (shortened) |
| Friends and behind-the-scenes | Observational, let it breathe, music bed, keep laughter | Daniel |
| The single biggest moment | Save it for the last third; tease it in the first 2 minutes | Joe, Yusufa |

## What none of them should teach you (collected)

- Third-party broadcast footage with the logo visible (Joe), borrowed lecture voiceover (Daniel), illustrated or AI allegory images inside observational docs (Omar).
- Their branding: "ZERO TO £100M" and NEXT RELEASE cards, the yellow "pov: 27 y/o" template, the logo outros, the Epidemic sponsor placement right after a climax.
- Multi-minute static monologues without cutaways (Daniel, Yusufa).
- "First person ever to see this" or disgust framing (Omar), and merging separate trips into one "journey" (Joe).
- Applying a heavy teal or saturation grade to footage that wasn't exposed for it.

## Confidence and gaps

- Frames are ~10 s apart, so cut points, transitions and camera moves aren't measured. Cut-rate estimates saturate near 18 cuts/min for Joe and Omar, which means only "brisk".
- Audio wasn't analysed. Music is inferred from speech gaps and caption markers.
- **Yusufa Sey:** 4 of 6 videos have no captions, so his speech numbers come from 2 videos; one of those opens on a rap track the captions treat as speech.
- **Omar Farooq:** language metrics come from English subtitles of Arabic speech. One transcript (Tunisia) has corrupted timing and is excluded automatically, and the Qatar video has none.
