# Advertising rules: GCC and Meta

Draft, 2026-11-20, from the verified advertising research. Not legal advice.
**[confirm with counsel]** marks open points. Primary texts not read: Meta's
policy pages, the UAE National Media Authority (NMA) site, the UAE legislation
PDFs, Kuwait Al-Youm. Read them before relying on any detail below.

## 1. Bottom line

- **Our own Meta ads** (B.V. brand page, paid through Ads Manager): no source
  shows a licence requirement in Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Bahrain or Oman for a
  foreign company's own paid ads. Two countries are open questions:
  - **UAE (counsel question #1):** the NMA Advertiser Permit exempts promoting
    your own business only, reportedly, where you hold a UAE trade licence. The
    B.V. has none. Do not run UAE-targeted ads until counsel answers.
  - **Kuwait (counsel question #2):** Decree-Law 10/2026 may require MOCI
    registration for digital commerce, which includes online advertising. Its
    in-force date is disputed. Do not run Kuwait-targeted ads until counsel answers.
- **Creators we pay need their own licence** (section 3). Do not hire creators
  in Qatar or Bahrain yet.
- **Health-ad permits** (MOHAP, DHA, DoH, SFDA, Kuwait MOH) should not be
  needed if we make no medical, dermatological, diagnostic, treatment or
  "scientifically proven" claims, name no products or devices, and never call
  reviewers doctors, experts or specialists. **[confirm with counsel per country]**
- **Biggest risks:** AI "after" images used as before/after creative, and copy
  that implies the viewer's appearance, health or weight.
- **Arabic:** run Arabic-first or bilingual creatives in every GCC ad set. A
  legal duty for Arabic *ads* is not confirmed anywhere; Arabic invoices are
  required in the UAE.

## 2. Do / don't for creatives and copy

## 4. Meta policy points

- **Personal attributes (confirmed):** ads may not assert or imply the
  viewer's health, medical condition or weight. Treat appearance-implying copy
  the same way. Read every "you/your" and «أنت/ـك».
- **Health & Wellness:** rewritten 22 Jul 2026 toward claim-by-claim review;
  sources disagree on before/after imagery. Our rule stays: no AI "after"
  images in ads.
- **18+ targeting** for the whole account (the service is 18+; Meta extended
  18+ targeting to weight-related products in the rewrite).
- **No Pixel or CAPI** at launch, so no custom audiences built from analysis
  results and no photo-flow events. If a Pixel is ever added: consent banner
  first, generic events only.
- **AI disclosure:** disclose AI-generated people or images in Ads Manager and
  in the creative.
- Special ad categories do not apply; do not use sensitive interest targeting.

## 5. Pre-flight checklist (every ad)

1. [ ] Targeting: 18+; only countries where checkout is open; no sensitive or custom audiences from analysis data.
2. [ ] Copy does not imply the viewer's appearance, health, weight or age (check every "you/your" and «أنت/ـك»).
3. [ ] No medical words (treat, cure, diagnose, doctor, dermatologist, clinic, علاج، طبي، دكتور، أخصائي).
4. [ ] No "scientifically proven", "guaranteed", or timeframe promises ("in 30 days").
5. [ ] No before/after transformation imagery and no AI "after" images.
6. [ ] Any AI-generated person or image is disclosed in Ads Manager and labelled in the creative (EN and AR).
7. [ ] No shirtless, underwear or suggestive imagery; no women; no flags, rulers, public figures or religious symbols.
8. [ ] Arabic version exists (default for every GCC ad set; legal duty unconfirmed); EN and AR say the same thing.
9. [ ] Price, currency, VAT wording ("incl. VAT" only in KSA, UAE, Bahrain, Oman), "30 days' membership included" and renewal terms match checkout exactly.
10. [ ] No competitor names, logos or comparisons.
11. [ ] Testimonials are real, with written consent on file; no customer photos or results without separate written marketing consent.
12. [ ] Landing page matches the ad (same claims, same price) and shows the B.V. name, terms, privacy policy and Arabic version.
13. [ ] Creator ads: licence or permit number recorded (KSA Mawthooq / UAE NMA permit / Kuwait licence when available); KSA agency licence checked; traceable payment; contract signed; records kept 5 years.
14. [ ] No Pixel or CAPI events; nothing about appearance or health leaves the site.
15. [ ] Log kept: ad ID, countries, copy, creative, approval date, approver (keep 5 years).
16. [ ] Kuwait-targeted ads: counsel has cleared the DL 10/2026 MOCI registration question.
17. [ ] UAE-targeted ads: counsel has cleared the Advertiser Permit question for the B.V.
18. [ ] Name used is the filed trademark form, with "™" or nothing (never "®" before registration).

## 6. Questions for counsel

1. UAE: does the NMA Advertiser Permit (or any NMA licence) apply to a foreign company without a UAE trade licence running paid Meta ads from its brand page?
2. Kuwait: is DL 10/2026 in force, and must a foreign seller register with MOCI before advertising or selling? Does an "advertised service must be licensed" rule block creator marketing?
3. Are non-medical appearance analysis and a grooming plan outside the health-advertising regimes in the UAE, KSA and Kuwait? Which words bring them in?
4. Can AI "after" illustrations be shown on the site under GCC misleading-ad rules, and with what label?
5. UAE CD 66/2023: is Arabic mandatory for advertisements, or only for invoices, warranties and contracts?
6. KSA: status of the new media law; does GAMR licensing reach brand accounts?
7. Arabic-language duties in KSA, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain and Oman.
8. Qatar and Bahrain creator licensing.
9. Comparative advertising, testimonials and endorsement disclosure per country.

## 7. Current vs pending (as of 2026-11-20)

- **Current:** UAE Media Law and regulations, administered by the NMA since ~Jan 2026 (Cabinet Res. 42/2025); Advertiser Permit mandatory since 1 Feb 2026; UAE consumer regulation (CD 66/2023, since 14 Oct 2023); KSA Mawthooq, E-Commerce Law, Audiovisual Media Law (2017); Meta Health & Wellness rewrite (22 Jul 2026).
- **Disputed:** Kuwait DL 10/2026 (in force since ~Sep 2026, or pending Executive Regulations).
- **Pending:** Kuwait DL 102/2026 (~Apr 2027, then regulations and a 6-month creator grace period); KSA comprehensive media law (no enactment found); Meta AI-disclosure rules for ads (unconfirmed).
