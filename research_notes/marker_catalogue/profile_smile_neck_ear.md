# Marker catalogue: side profile, 45°, smile, neck and ear

Scope: markers measured from a **strict side-profile** photo, a **45° (three-quarter)** photo, a **posed-smile** photo, and **neck** and **ear** markers. This is the part of the catalogue covering what we do not measure today. Compiled 2026-11-10.

**Count:** 90 markers: profile/global 12, nose 18, lips 12, chin 5, jaw 4, neck 9, ear 12, smile 14, 45° view 4. Qoves lists Nose 17, Lips 16, Chin 8, Jaw 11, Smile 13, Neck 11, Ear 12. Our counts are not split the same way, because Qoves counts some frontal lip, chin and jaw items that belong in other files.

## How to read this file

- **Norm values** are copied as published (mean ± SD, n, units). "not found" means we did not find a value in a source we could open. We have not estimated or filled in any value.
- **Cephalometric vs photographic norms.** Most published profile norms come from lateral **cephalograms**. Soft-tissue points on a cephalogram match the photo outline closely, but magnification (about 8–10% on cephalograms) and head orientation differ. Treat cephalometric **mm** values as approximate on photos. **Angles and ratios** carry over better. Photogrammetric norms are marked "(photo)".
- **Scale.** Any mm value on a photo needs calibration, for example a ruler or sticker in the frame, or a known distance taken from the frontal capture. Angles and ratios need no scale.
- **Reference lines.** Frankfort horizontal (FH) needs porion and orbitale, which sit inside the silhouette and are unreliable on photos. Where a marker uses FH, we propose **true horizontal/vertical (TH/TV)**. This needs a level camera and natural head position (NHP) at capture. Saadeh 2025 and Celebi 2013 also took their cephalograms in NHP.
- **Automatable codes:**
  - **OUT**: needs only the profile silhouette outline, which we can segment against the plain backdrop. Includes points that lie on the outline: G, N', Prn, Cm (columella), Sn, Ls, Stms/Stmi (lip-contact notch), Li, Sm/B', Pg', Gn', Me', C (cervical point) and the throat contour.
  - **OUT+TV**: needs the outline plus a true vertical or horizontal from a level camera.
  - **LMK**: needs landmarks or segmentation inside the silhouette, such as the alar crease, vermilion border, ear, teeth, gingiva, gonial shading or eye.
  - **REV**: needs reviewer judgement or a categorical grading.
  - **[mm]**: needs scale calibration.
- **Landmark abbreviations (soft tissue):**
  - Upper face: Tr (trichion), G (glabella), N' (soft-tissue nasion).
  - Nose: Prn (pronasale, nasal tip), SupT (supratip point), Cm (columella point), Sn (subnasale), AC (alar crease).
  - Lips and chin: Ls/Li (labrale superius/inferius), Stms/Stmi (stomion superius/inferius), Sm (soft-tissue B point / mentolabial sulcus), Pg' (soft-tissue pogonion), Gn' (soft-tissue gnathion), Me' (soft-tissue menton).
  - Neck: C (cervical point, the deepest point of the throat-neck junction). Ellenbogen's "subhyoid depression" is also at this junction.
  - Ear: sa/sba (superaurale/subaurale), pra/pa (preaurale/postaurale).
  - Smile: Ch (cheilion, mouth corner).

## Sources used and access notes

**Primary analyses.** Values for these come from abstracts or from secondary tables in open-access papers, unless a note says otherwise:

- Lip and profile lines: Ricketts 1968 (E-line), Steiner (S-line), Burstone 1967 (Sn–Pg' line).
- Profile angles: Holdaway 1983/84 (H-angle), Legan & Burstone 1980 (via Celebi 2013 Table 1, "European-American" column), Merrifield 1966 (Z-angle).
- Nose and chin: Powell & Humphreys 1984 (aesthetic triangle; secondary sources only), Gonzalez-Ulloa 1968, Crumley & Lanser 1988, Byrd & Hobar 1993.
- Neck: Ellenbogen & Karlin 1980 (the abstract does not list the criteria; the 105–120° angle comes via O'Daniel 2026).
- Facial analysis: Arnett & Bergman 1993, Arnett 1999, Bergman 1999.
- Smile: Sarver 2001, Sarver & Ackerman 2003, Ackerman 1998, Kokich 1999/2006, Moore 2005, Parekh 2007, Ioi 2005/2009, Tjan 1984, Vig & Brundo 1978, Johnston 1999.
- Duchenne smile: Ekman 1990, Frank 1993.
- Perception thresholds: the Naini et al. series on mentolabial, nasofrontal, nasofacial, upper-lip inclination, E-line, submental-cervical angle, submental length, lip–chin angle and profile-smile incisor inclination.

**Arab / Gulf / Middle-Eastern norm sources found:**

- **Saudi:**
  - AlBarakati 2011, Legan–Burstone soft-tissue norms, n = 61. Values via Celebi 2013 Table 3; values are pooled, not split by sex.
  - Aljabaa 2019, nasal cephalometric norms, n = 62, open access, full tables read.
  - Uysal 2011, Saudi vs Turkish. Publisher blocks full text; E-line and H-angle values via Saadeh 2025 Table 5.
  - Hashim & AlBarakati 2003, abstract only.
  - Hiware 2024, ear morphometry, Eastern Province, n = 200, open access.
  - Ismail 2026, smile parameters, Jouf University, n = 200, open access.
  - Ganem 2026, smile-arc prevalence, n = 150.
- **Emirati:** Abutayyem 2021, n = 176, open access, full tables read. Caution: several female values look internally inconsistent, for example upper-lip length F 16.8 vs M 27.08 mm, N'-Sn-Pg' F 133.8° and upper-lip protrusion F 12.01 mm. Use with care.
- **Kuwaiti, Qatari and other Emirati samples:** values only via Saadeh 2025 Table 5. We did not verify the original papers.
- **Lebanese:** Saadeh 2025, open access, full table read. The abstract says n = 165 (59 M / 106 F) but the tables say n = 205 (99 M / 106 F). This is the authors' inconsistency.
- **Turkish:** Celebi 2013, Legan–Burstone, n = 96, open access.
- **Iranian:** Rakhshan & Ghorbanyjavadpour 2019 (abstract only, no numbers in abstract), Mohebbi 2016 (tip-projection preference), Golfeshan 2024 (lip-position acceptance).
- **Syrian:** Torbey 2024, nasal photogrammetry, n = 100, open access. Mortada 2023, attractiveness in Class II.
- **Yemeni:** Al-Gunaid 2007, via Celebi 2013 Table 3.

**Gulf or Arab perception (attractiveness) studies found:**

- Sadek & Alali 2025: Middle-Eastern raters prefer lips 1 mm ahead of the Ricketts norm.
- Alshammari 2023 (Saudi): lip position on silhouettes.
- Albwardi 2022 (Saudi): incisor inclination in the profile smile.
- Almutairi 2015 (Saudi): bimaxillary protrusion seen in frontal and three-quarter smile views.
- Talic 2013 (Saudi): altered smile features.
- Alaqeely 2023 and Alfaifi 2026 (Saudi): gingival display.

**Inaccessible or not read in full:**

- pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov is blocked in this environment. We used Europe PMC and the NCBI E-utilities instead.
- Publisher blocks full-text XML for Uysal 2011 (doi:10.1016/j.sdentj.2011.08.002) and Hussein 2011, a Palestinian Holdaway norms study (doi:10.1016/j.sdentj.2011.08.003).
- Al Taki 2018 (Circassian, Jordan, Legan–Burstone) and Raghav 2014 have their tables as images. Ismail 2026 Tables 1–3 are in the PDF only; the values below come from the results text.
- Books not accessed: Powell & Humphreys 1984, Naini 2011, Farkas 1994, and Farkas 1987 (otoplastic architecture).
- Not accessed: the full texts of Ricketts 1968, Holdaway 1983, Legan & Burstone 1980 (J Oral Surg, no DOI), Ellenbogen & Karlin 1980, Arnett 1999, Vig & Brundo 1978, Tjan 1984 and Farkas 2005.
- Talic & Al-Shakhs 2008 (Saudi profile attractiveness, Saudi Dent J 20:17–23) has no abstract or full text indexed.

**Main gaps:**

1. No **Gulf-specific** norms exist for nasal tip projection ratios (Goode, Crumley, Simons), cervicomental or submental-cervical angle, gonial angle on soft tissue, ear axis or protrusion, buccal corridor perception, or any 45° measure.
2. Emirati norms have consistency problems, and Saudi Legan–Burstone values are pooled, not split by sex, in the copy we could read.
3. We found no quantitative norms for the ogee curve, malar prominence in the 45° view, jawline definition, neck length, forehead inclination or columella show.
4. Most perception thresholds (Naini) were tested on a single **white male or female silhouette** with UK raters. Only the lip-position, incisor-inclination and smile studies above used Arab raters.

**Count: 49 markers can be computed from the outline alone or with the outline plus TV**: profile 12, nose 14, lips 11, chin 5, neck 7. jaw-mandibular-plane-soft is partly outline-based. Ear, smile, gonial-angle, alar-crease and columella markers all need landmarks inside the silhouette, and 45° markers need reviewer judgement.

**Practical notes for the outline:**

- C (cervical point) needs the head in natural position. A raised chin opens the cervicomental angle.
- Stms and Stmi need the lips relaxed and closed or at rest.
- Hair, beard, hijab or shemagh can hide G/Tr, the submental outline and the ear. This matters for Gulf capture and needs a capture protocol or exclusion flags.
