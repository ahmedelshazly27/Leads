# Frontal-view facial marker catalogue (literature-backed)

Scope: markers that can be measured from **one frontal photograph**: overall proportions, eyes, eyebrows, nose, lips, cheeks, jaw/chin and ears seen from the front. Markers that need a profile, oblique or basal view appear only where a frontal proxy exists, and the table says so. Compiled 2026-11-09/10 for the Gulf (UAE/KSA) launch.

## How to read this

- **Norms** are given as mean ± SD (n) when the source gives them. "Ratio of means" is a ratio we computed from two published group means. It is **not** a published individual-level ratio and has no SD. We mark it "(RoM)". "Derived" means we computed a value from a published difference column (used for Farkas North-American-White [NAW] values taken from Virdi 2019).
- **mm vs ratio.** Our photos have no scale, so ratios are preferred. Every mm norm can still be used once a photo is scaled with the iris (see `gen-iris-mm-scale`): published scaling uses a fixed corneal ("white-to-white") diameter of 11.70 mm [R22, R15] or a pooled mean of 11.84 ± 0.79 mm [R23].
- **Automatable:** `auto-ratio` = computed from 2D landmarks with no scale. `auto-mm` = automatic but needs iris-based mm calibration. `review` = needs reviewer judgement, or a landmark that MediaPipe does not provide reliably.
- **MediaPipe indices** follow the mapping already used in `app/src/lib/faceAnalysis.ts` (`IDX`, 478-point mesh with iris refinement; "L" = image-left = subject's right). They are only listed where that mapping or the canonical mesh gives a clear point. Points marked "≈" are approximations of the anthropometric landmark: for example, 234/454 lie on the face contour near tragus level rather than at true soft-tissue zygion, 10 is upper forehead rather than trichion, 168 is near nasion, and 172/397 are near gonion. Alare is taken as the max of 129/358, 64/294 and 98/327, as in the code. **No MediaPipe point gives trichion (hairline), soft-tissue zygion, gonion, superaurale/subaurale (ears) or maxillofrontale reliably.**
- "Existing" in a row means the app already computes something equivalent (`faceAnalysis.ts` `computeShape`). For those rows the value of this file is the evidence-based norm.

## Gaps and caveats (read before using any norm)

1. **No Emirati-specific frontal facial norms were found.** Gulf data come from Saudi (and Yemeni) studies. The only "Gulf Arabs" figure (Dharap 2013, alar width) is known only through Al-Sebaei's table [R2]. Kuwait/Qatar/Bahrain/Oman: none found.
2. **Head coverings.** For many Gulf users the hairline (trichion), forehead and ears are covered (hijab, niqab, ghutra). Markers that need trichion (classic thirds, physiognomic index, Pallett's face length) or ears should degrade gracefully to "not measurable". The Middle-East consensus [R7] notes that veiling changes which features matter: eyes dominate for niqab wearers, and temple fullness matters under a headscarf.
3. **The neoclassical canons fail in Arab samples.** In attractive Saudi students the vertical thirds were unequal, en-en < al-al in 92.4% of men and 56% of women, and ex-en > en-en in most subjects [R2]. Any "ideal = 1" target (eye spacing, nose = intercanthal) is not a valid Gulf norm.
4. **Inconsistencies inside sources** (flagged in the rows below):
   - Al-Sebaei [R2]: the abstract says 93 M / 75 F, but Table 1 is headed "Males N = 75, Females N = 93". The abstract also says "wider intercanthal distance than eye fissure length", while the table and the means show the opposite.
   - The Farkas 2005 Iranian eye values reproduced in R2 look implausible (en-ex 37.2 M vs 24.4 F).
   - The Persian study [R6] has typographical errors in its tables (e.g. go-go "18.29 ± 70.7"; upper vermilion > lower vermilion, which contradicts most data).
   - The Saudi 3D study [R4] reports implausible facial-width differences, and its 3D widths run higher than caliper widths.
5. **Not accessed** (paywall or blocked network): Farkas 1994 book and the Farkas 2005 tables, Naini 2011 book, Powell & Humphreys 1984 book, full texts of Pallett 2010 and Popenko 2017 (abstracts only), Schmid 2008, Cunningham 1986, Swift & Remington 2011 (abstract has no numbers), and the full texts of the Arab nose/lip/ear papers above. The host pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov and most publisher sites were unreachable from this session; Europe PMC open-access full texts were used instead.
6. Normative means describe *average* faces. Only rows with a "perception" entry have evidence linking the measure to attractiveness, sex-typicality or age.
