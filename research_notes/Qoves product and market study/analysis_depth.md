## 1. Metrics and features (front vs profile)
### Takeaway
Cephalometric/anthropometric framework: soft-tissue landmark measurements compared to population/ethnic norms, plus "evidence-based guide" constructs (averageness, symmetry, dimorphism, neoteny, facial adiposity, harmony, proportionality), skin/aging cues and a "First Impression" score. Profile: E-line, nasolabial angle, gonial angle, forehead/chin projection, smile profile.
### Cited findings
- Claims 521 facial points and 160+ "beauty markers"; another review cites 112 parameters (inconsistent). — realworldappeal; thefaceanalysis
- Workflow: computer-vision landmarking then specialist human review; each region broken into within-norm vs deviating. — realworldappeal
- Measurement library pages: eye spacing ratio, canthal tilt, gonial angle, lower gonial angle, facial thirds, Ricketts E-line, nasolabial angle, upper:lower lip ratio. — https://www.qoves.com/insights/measurements
- Nasolabial angle "healthy" 90–110° (women a few degrees higher); population means 97.9° (Turkish males) to 104.3° (Han Chinese males); no single global ideal. — qoves.com/insights/measurements/nasolabial-angle
- Gonial angle 118–130° men, 118–126° women; separate "lower gonial angle" ~70–75°. — qoves.com/insights/measurements/gonial-angle; …/lower-gonial-angle
- Sample Comprehensive report (Angel Olavarria): "cephalometric"; definitions credit Farkas (1994) and Powell (1984); facial thirds ≈ equal (male lower third may exceed middle); transverse symmetry; malar prominence vs "ideal 15–20 mm" (Powell); canthal tilt per eye (+4°) vs ~+7° for attractive Caucasian faces; under-eye hollows/dark circles flagged as premature aging. — Studocu / looksmax PDF
- Sample Preliminary report: measurements vs Caucasian averages; "slightly receded" forehead, chin "lacks prominence". — Scribd Preliminary V3
- Report areas: proportions, symmetry, dentofacial/teeth-jaw relationship, midface/jaw, eyes, nose, lips, profile; Comprehensive adds smile profile and facial development. — Scribd; v1.qoves.com Comprehensive
- Guide pages exist for averageness, symmetry, dimorphism, neoteny, adiposity, harmony, proportionality (qoves.com/insights/...). Dimorphism: full lips, prominent cheekbones read feminine; neoteny: dark circles, thinning lips, midface volume loss, receding hairline read as aging; adiposity: facial fat masks cheekbones, thins lips.
- "Facial Masculinity"/"Facial Femininity" measures and "First Impression" score on site; no "harshness" metric found.
- Skin glossary: under-eye health, pigmentation. — qoves.com/welcome
### Gaps
fWHR, scleral show, upper-eyelid exposure, brow position, nasal dorsum/tip, ramus, skin texture score not confirmed as named metrics; current 2026 report outline not found; score computation undocumented.

## 3. Literature
- Articles cite Perrett et al. 1998 (Nature), Rhodes 2006, Langlois & Roggman 1990 (and critiques), Langlois et al. 2000 meta-analysis, Re & Perrett 2014; reports cite Farkas 1994, Powell 1984.
- Guardian (5 Aug 2026, via secondary coverage): a cited researcher said his findings don't generalise across populations; a tipping statistic traced to Parrett 2015 (J Economic Psychology).
- Product claims without independent evidence found: red-light eye mask, beta-carotene.
- Newer cross-cultural evidence a competitor can cite: 2023 five-population study (averageness and dimorphism poor predictors; dimorphism link perhaps universal only for women) — PMC10427909; fWHR sex differences equivocal — PMC9066300.

## 6. Criticism and timeline
- MIT Technology Review (5 Mar 2021): opaque algorithms, training-data bias (darker skin, wider noses penalised); anchoring (Rhue).
- AIAAIC incident (Mar 2021): accuracy and bias by race/ethnicity/age.
- Guardian (5 Aug 2026, secondary): BDD/eating-disorder risk; bypassable age/self-report screening; Eurocentric imagery; Qoves didn't comment.
- Timeline: 2021 free AI tool + paid reports; one-time era Preliminary $150/10pp, Tertiary $250/16pp, Comprehensive $300/24pp, earlier $35 basic; prices $300 (Jul 2023), $160 (Mar 2026), $175 (Apr/Jun 2026); Sep–Oct 2026 $150/yr membership, one analysis/yr, v1 store retired.
### Competitor inferences
Publish method and reference datasets; report measurement error; ethnicity/sex/age-stratified norms with sample sizes; validated BDD screening (e.g. BDDQ); separate measurement from attractiveness claims.
